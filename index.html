Turn sound into something you can see.

What's actually running under the hood

REAL-TIME

Audio-reactive engine

Analyzes frequency and transients as they happen — nothing is pre-rendered.

CONTROL

MIDI & OSC mapping

Map any control surface to a visual parameter in a few clicks.

LIBRARY

40+ presets

Start from a preset and push it somewhere new — every preset exposes its parameters.

OUTPUT

Stream-ready output

Sends straight to OBS, a projector, or a second display with no capture card needed.
